Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

You can cancel a booking from the Bookings tab in the app. Here is what happens at each stage.

On this page
  1. Cancelling a booking
  2. If we or the professional cancel
  3. Refunds
  4. Poor quality or incomplete work
  5. Coupons
  6. How to reach us

1. Cancelling a booking

  • Before a professional accepts: free cancellation.
  • After acceptance, more than 2 hours before the start time: free cancellation.
  • Less than 2 hours before the start time, or after the professional is on the way: a visit charge of up to Rs 100 may apply to cover travel.
  • After work has started: you pay for the work already done.

2. If we or the professional cancel

If a professional cancels or does not arrive, you pay nothing. We will try to assign another professional. If we cannot, the booking is cancelled at no cost to you.

3. Refunds

Most bookings are paid directly to the professional after the work. If you paid in advance for a booking that was cancelled under this policy, the money is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

4. Poor quality or incomplete work

Tell us within 48 hours of the job through Help and support in the app, with photos if you can. We will ask the professional to redo the work. If it cannot be fixed, we will work with you and the partner company on a fair partial or full refund of the amount paid for the affected work.

5. Coupons

If a booking made with a coupon is cancelled before the service, the coupon is returned where it is still valid.

6. How to reach us

Email easyandroid77@gmail.com or call +91 70029 37879 with your booking details. We reply within 2 working days.
