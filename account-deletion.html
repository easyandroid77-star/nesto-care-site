Delete your Nesto Care account

Effective 12 November 2026

You can delete your account yourself from the app, or ask us to do it. This page applies to the Nesto Care customer app and the Nesto Care Partner app.

On this page
  1. Delete from the app (fastest)
  2. Cannot open the app? Ask us to delete it
  3. What is deleted
  4. What is kept
  5. After deletion

1. Delete from the app (fastest)

  • Open the Nesto Care app
  • Tap Account at the bottom
  • Tap Delete account
  • Read the message and tap Delete to confirm

Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out. In the Partner app the same option is under Account for staff members. A company admin should email us instead, so that the company's team and bookings are handled properly.

2. Cannot open the app? Ask us to delete it

Email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my Nesto Care account" and tell us your name and the mobile number you used. We may ask a question to confirm it is you. We complete the deletion within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number and profile photo
  • Saved addresses and favourites
  • Notifications and support messages
  • Your login and device tokens, so no more notifications are sent

4. What is kept

  • Booking, invoice and payment records are kept in anonymised form, without your name, number or address, for up to 3 years or as the law requires, for accounting, tax and dispute handling
  • If a booking you made is still in progress, we may complete the record first

If you have made no bookings, your account is removed completely.

5. After deletion

Deleted accounts cannot be restored. You can start fresh at any time by opening the app again and booking with your number.
