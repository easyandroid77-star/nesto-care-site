2. Data we collect

Customer app

  • Name and mobile number, which you enter when you book a service
  • Service addresses you save, including house details, landmark, town and pincode
  • Booking details: services chosen, date and time, notes you write, price, status and ratings you give
  • Approximate or precise location, only when you tap the option to use your current location while adding an address
  • A random sign-in identifier that keeps you logged in on your device
  • Device push token, so we can send booking updates

Partner app (company admin and staff)

  • Name, mobile number, username and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form and cannot be read by us
  • Profile photo, if you add one
  • Jobs assigned to you, their status, notes and invoices
  • Device information needed to show job alerts, including a foreground service notification that keeps alerts running while you are online

We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone recordings or payment card details. We do not show third party ads.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your bookings and show them to the right professional
  • To let the professional contact you and reach your address
  • To send booking updates, reminders and job alerts
  • To run your account, keep you logged in and keep the service secure
  • To handle support requests, complaints and disputes
  • To improve the app and fix problems
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

4. Why we may process it

We process data you give us because you asked for a service, because you agreed when you used the app, and where the law requires us to keep records. You can withdraw consent for optional items, such as location and notifications, in your phone settings at any time.

5. Who can see your data

  • The professional assigned to your booking sees your name, mobile number, address and booking details so they can do the job. They see them only for that booking.
  • The company admin of the partner company that provides the service can see bookings handled by their staff.
  • Service providers that run our systems. Our database, login and file storage run on Supabase, which processes data on our behalf under its own security terms.
  • Authorities where the law requires or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Permissions the apps ask for

  • Location: to fill in your address when you choose "use my location". Optional.
  • Camera and photos: in the Partner app, to add a profile or catalog photo. Optional.
  • Notifications: to tell you about bookings and jobs.
  • Internet access: needed to use the apps.
  • Foreground service (Partner app only): keeps job alerts running while staff are online. Staff can turn it off by going offline.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your profile, saved addresses and notifications while your account is active. When you delete your account these are removed. Booking, invoice and payment records may be kept in anonymised form for up to 3 years, or longer where the law requires, for accounting, tax and dispute handling. Our backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

8. Your rights and choices

  • See and correct your name, mobile number and addresses inside the app
  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent and stop notifications or location in your phone settings
  • Delete your account from the app. Go to Account, then Delete account. Full steps are on the account deletion page

You can also email easyandroid77@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to data is limited by account and role, so customers can see only their own bookings and staff can see only the work of their company. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and log out on shared phones.

10. Children

The apps are meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If the change is significant we also tell you in the app.
